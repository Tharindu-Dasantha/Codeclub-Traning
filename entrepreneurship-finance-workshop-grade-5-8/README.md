# 🚀 Code Club Workshop: From Idea to Impact — Entrepreneurship & Finance Basics
> **Target Audience:** Grade 5-8 Students (Ages 10-14 in Sri Lanka)
> **Duration:** 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
> **Topic:** Entrepreneurial Thinking, Basic Financial Literacy, and Pitching
> **Prerequisites:** No new technical skills required — this final session draws on everything built across the whole Code Club term!

---

## 📌 Workshop Overview

Welcome to **From Idea to Impact** — the **final session** of our Code Club journey! This 2-hour interactive workshop is designed specifically for Sri Lankan Grade 5-8 students in Code Club.

Over the term, students have learned to build (HTML), style (CSS), think logically (Python), sense the physical world (Arduino), and create responsibly with AI. Today, they learn the final, powerful skill: **turning an idea into a plan people would actually want** — the foundation of entrepreneurship. Students brainstorm a real problem worth solving, learn basic **financial literacy** (revenue, cost, profit, pricing), refine their idea using AI (safely and critically, as covered in the AI workshop), build a one-page **mini business plan**, and deliver a **"Dragon's Den" style mini-pitch** to their peers.

This session isn't really about becoming a business owner overnight — it's about learning to think in problems, solutions, numbers, and confident communication — skills useful in any future career, technical or otherwise.

## 🎯 Key Learning Objectives

By the end of this 2-hour session, students will be able to:
1. Explain entrepreneurial thinking as **finding a real problem and designing a solution someone would value** — not just "having a cool idea."
2. Use the **Problem → Solution → Customer → Unique Selling Point** framework to describe a business idea clearly.
3. Understand and calculate basic financial literacy concepts: **Revenue**, **Cost**, **Profit** (Revenue − Cost), and how to set a fair **Price**.
4. Use AI as a critical-thinking partner (per the AI workshop's safety guidance) to refine their pitch wording and double-check their numbers — not to replace their own thinking.
5. Complete a one-page **Mini Business Plan** for their idea.
6. Deliver and receive feedback on a short, structured **"Dragon's Den" style pitch** in front of peers.

## 🛠️ Required Setup & Hardware

### For Students:
* Pen/paper or a text editor for the Mini Business Plan template (`ACTIVITIES.md`).
* Optional: access to the same facilitator-supervised AI tool(s) used in the AI Creativity Workshop, for the "Refine with AI" step.

### For Instructor / Facilitator:
* Projector or large display screen.
* Whiteboard & color markers.
* Printed copies of `STUDENT_CHEATSHEET.md` and the Mini Business Plan template from `ACTIVITIES.md`.
* **If reusing AI tools from the AI Creativity Workshop:** follow the same account/age-safety guidance from that session's `README.md` — one supervised, projected account rather than individual student logins, unless your organization has an approved education account.
* Optional: small prizes/certificates for the "Dragon's Den" pitch round, and a few adult volunteers or older students to help act as friendly "Dragons" (judges) if available.

## 🚀 How to Run This Session

1. **Before Class:**
   - Review `LESSON_PLAN.md` for timing benchmarks and `ACTIVITIES.md` for the Mini Business Plan template and pitch structure.
   - Print the Mini Business Plan template if possible.
   - Prepare 2-3 relatable local example business ideas (a food stall, a tuition/tutoring service, a recycling or upcycling business, an app idea) to use in Module 1's discussion.
2. **During Class:**
   - Follow the 120-minute timeline in `LESSON_PLAN.md`.
   - Project `SLIDES.md` on the main screen for visual explanation.
   - Facilitate brainstorming, the financial literacy exercise, the Mini Business Plan, and the Dragon's Den pitch round from `ACTIVITIES.md`.
3. **After Class:**
   - Celebrate — this is the final session of the term! Consider a small closing ceremony recognizing every student's journey across all sessions.
