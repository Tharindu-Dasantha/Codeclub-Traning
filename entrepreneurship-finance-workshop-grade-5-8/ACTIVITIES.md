# 🎮 Student Activities: Brainstorm, Business Plan & Dragon's Den Pitch

> **Workshop:** From Idea to Impact — Entrepreneurship & Finance Basics (Grade 5-8)
> **Instructions:** Complete Activity 1 to find your idea, Activity 2 to work out your numbers, Activity 3 to build your plan, then get ready to pitch in Activity 4!

---

## 💡 Activity 1: Brainstorm Your Idea

### Step 1: What Annoys You?
List 3 real problems or annoyances you or your family regularly face:

```text
1. ___________________________________________
2. ___________________________________________
3. ___________________________________________
```

Circle your favorite one to turn into a business idea.

### Step 2: The PSCU Framework
Fill in the framework for your chosen idea:

```text
PROBLEM:  What real problem are you solving?
__________________________________________________

SOLUTION: What is your product or service?
__________________________________________________

CUSTOMER: Who exactly would want/need this? (Be specific - not "everyone"!)
__________________________________________________

UNIQUE:   What makes yours different or better than existing options?
__________________________________________________
```

> 💭 **Inspiration:** Feeling stuck? Look back at what you built in Code Club — your quiz game, website, or Arduino circuit could be the seed of a real idea!

## 💰 Activity 2: Work Out Your Numbers

Using rough, realistic estimates, fill in the numbers for your idea:

```text
PRICE (what you charge for ONE unit):        Rs. _______

COST (what it costs YOU to make ONE unit):   Rs. _______

PROFIT PER UNIT (Price − Cost):              Rs. _______

If you sold 10 units:
   Total Revenue (10 × Price):               Rs. _______
   Total Cost    (10 × Cost):                Rs. _______
   Total Profit  (Revenue − Cost):           Rs. _______
```

### 🏆 Bonus: Break-Even Challenge
If you needed to spend a one-time amount of money to get started (e.g., buying tools or ingredients in bulk), work out how many units you'd need to sell just to cover that cost:

```text
One-time starting cost:                      Rs. _______
Break-even units = starting cost ÷ profit per unit = _______ units
```

### 🤖 Refine with AI
Using the same facilitator-supervised AI tool from the AI Creativity Workshop, try a prompt like:
```text
"Here is my business idea: [describe it]. Can you suggest one way to make
my pitch more exciting, and check if my profit math looks correct:
I sell for Rs. ___, my cost is Rs. ___, so my profit is Rs. ___ per item."
```
Remember: read any AI suggestion critically. Does the math actually check out? Does it still sound like YOUR idea? You decide what to keep.

## 🎤 Activity 4: Prepare & Deliver Your Dragon's Den Pitch

### The Pitch Structure (60-90 seconds):
```text
1. HOOK        → A question or bold statement to grab attention
2. PROBLEM     → The real problem you're solving
3. SOLUTION    → Your product/service, briefly
4. CUSTOMER    → Who this is for
5. THE NUMBERS → One key number (your price, or your profit per item)
6. THE ASK     → What you want from your "Dragons" - feedback, belief in
                 the idea, or a pretend investment
```

### Practice in Your Pitch Pod:
1. Form a group of 4-5 students.
2. Take turns pitching (60-90 seconds each) while the rest of the pod plays "Dragons."
3. After each pitch, the pod gives ONE encouraging comment and ONE curious question.
4. Vote on your pod's favorite pitch to potentially share with the whole class!
