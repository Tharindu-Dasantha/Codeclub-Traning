## 💡 What Makes a Good Business Idea?

1. It solves a **real problem** for **real people**.
2. Someone would actually be willing to **pay** for it.
3. It's something **you** could realistically start, even in a small way.

---

## 🧩 The PSCU Framework

```text
PROBLEM   → What real problem are you solving?
SOLUTION  → What is your product or service?
CUSTOMER  → Who exactly would want/need this? (Not "everyone"!)
UNIQUE    → What makes yours different or better?
```

---

## 💰 The 4 Financial Literacy Basics

| Term | Meaning | Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue** | Total money coming IN from sales | Price × Number Sold |
| **Cost** | Total money going OUT to make/run it | Cost per unit × Number Made |
| **Profit** | What you actually keep | Revenue − Cost |
| **Price** | What you charge for ONE unit | Set by you |

### Worked Example
```text
Price per bracelet:     Rs. 150
Cost per bracelet:      Rs. 50
Profit per bracelet:    Rs. 150 - Rs. 50 = Rs. 100

Sell 20 bracelets:
  Total Revenue:  20 x Rs. 150 = Rs. 3,000
  Total Cost:     20 x Rs. 50  = Rs. 1,000
  Total Profit:   Rs. 3,000 - Rs. 1,000 = Rs. 2,000
```

### Bonus: Break-Even
```text
Break-even units = Fixed (one-time) cost ÷ Profit per unit
```

## 🎤 The Dragon's Den Pitch Structure (60-90 seconds)

```text
1. HOOK        → Grab attention with a question or bold statement
2. PROBLEM     → The real problem you're solving
3. SOLUTION    → Your product/service, briefly
4. CUSTOMER    → Who this is for
5. THE NUMBERS → One key number (price or profit per item)
6. THE ASK     → What you want from your "Dragons"
```

## 🤖 Using AI to Refine Your Idea (Recap from the AI Workshop)

- Use only the facilitator-supervised/approved AI tool.
- Ask AI to suggest ways to make your pitch clearer or more exciting.
- Ask AI to double-check your math — then verify it yourself. AI can make mistakes too!
- You decide what to keep. If a suggestion doesn't feel like your idea anymore, don't use it.

---

## 🚨 Top 4 Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ **A vague customer** — "Everyone" is not a real target customer.
2. ❌ **No clear problem** — If there's no real problem, there's no real reason to buy.
3. ❌ **Ignoring cost** — A great idea that costs more to make than it sells for isn't profitable yet.
4. ❌ **Trusting AI's math blindly** — Always double-check Revenue − Cost = Profit yourself.

---

## ⌨️ Useful Tips

- Keep your numbers simple and round — the goal is understanding the relationship between price, cost, and profit, not perfect accounting.
- Practice your pitch out loud at least once before presenting — it always sounds different than it does in your head!
- A great pitch is confident AND honest — it's okay to say "I'm still figuring this part out."
