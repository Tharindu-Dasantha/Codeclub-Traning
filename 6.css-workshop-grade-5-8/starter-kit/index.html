About Me & My Game

Hello! My name is Kasun. I am in Grade 6 and I built Jungle Maze Escape in Scratch — a game where an elephant escapes a jungle maze while dodging a leopard!

How to Play

  • Use the Arrow Keys to move the elephant
  • Avoid touching the leopard sprite
  • Collect bananas for bonus points
  • Reach the exit gate to win!
