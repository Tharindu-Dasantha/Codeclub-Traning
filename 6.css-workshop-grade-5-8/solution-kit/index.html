About Me & My Game

Hello! Welcome to my digital corner. I am a Grade 6 student in Colombo, Sri Lanka. In Jungle Maze Escape, you help a baby elephant escape a jungle maze while dodging a sneaky leopard and collecting bananas!

How to Play

  • ⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️
    Move the Elephant

    Use the Arrow Keys to walk through the maze.

  • 🐆
    Dodge the Leopard

    Touching the leopard sprite sends you back to the start!

  • 🚪
    Reach the Exit

    Find the glowing exit gate to win the level.
