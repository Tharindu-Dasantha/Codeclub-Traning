# 🛡️ Code Club Workshop: Be Safe Online!
> **Target Audience:** Grade 5-8 Students (Ages 10-14 in Sri Lanka)
> **Duration:** 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
> **Topic:** Internet Safety, Recognizing Online Threats, and Responsible Digital Habits
> **Prerequisites:** None! This session is for every student, regardless of coding experience.

---

## 📌 Workshop Overview

Welcome to **Be Safe Online**! This 2-hour interactive workshop is designed specifically for Sri Lankan Grade 5-8 students in Code Club.

Every student who has built a webpage or a Python game in Code Club is now spending real time online — and the internet is an amazing place to learn, create, and connect, but it also has real risks: scams, strangers, cyberbullying, and tricks designed to steal information. This session is **not about coding** — it's about giving students the judgment to navigate the internet safely and confidently.

Through relatable analogies (the internet as a giant city with safe streets and dangerous alleys), real-example "Spot the Scam" rounds, group discussion of tricky scenarios, and a fun interactive quiz game, students learn 5 Golden Rules they can apply immediately — at home, on their phones, and on any device they use.

## 🎯 Key Learning Objectives

By the end of this 2-hour session, students will be able to:
1. Explain, in their own words, what personal information should **never** be shared publicly online.
2. Create and recognize a **strong password**, and explain why passwords should never be reused or shared.
3. Identify the common **red flags of scams and phishing messages** (urgency, "too good to be true" offers, suspicious links, spelling mistakes in web addresses).
4. Describe what **cyberbullying** looks like and the correct response: don't engage, save evidence, tell a trusted adult.
5. Recognize the warning signs of an online stranger who isn't who they claim to be, and know never to meet an online-only contact in person alone.
6. Apply the **STOP → BLOCK → TELL** framework when something online feels wrong or scary.
7. Apply the **T.H.I.N.K.** test (True? Helpful? Inspiring? Necessary? Kind?) before posting or sharing anything online.

## 🛠️ Required Setup & Hardware

### For Students:
* Desktop/Laptop/tablet with any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge) to play the quiz game.
* *No internet connection required* — `interactive-demo/playground.html` runs entirely offline, no installs needed.
* Pen/pencil for the Spot-the-Scam worksheet and Safety Pledge in `ACTIVITIES.md`.

### For Instructor / Facilitator:
* Projector or large display screen (to project scenarios and the quiz game for group discussion).
* Whiteboard & color markers.
* Printed copies of `STUDENT_CHEATSHEET.md` and the Safety Pledge from `ACTIVITIES.md` (recommended — great for students to take home and stick on a wall).

---

## 🚀 How to Run This Session

1. **Before Class:**
   - Open `interactive-demo/playground.html` in a browser to confirm it works on the lab computers/projector.
   - Review `LESSON_PLAN.md` for timing benchmarks and `ACTIVITIES.md` for the Spot-the-Scam examples.
   - Print the Safety Pledge worksheet if possible.
2. **During Class:**
   - Follow the 120-minute timeline in `LESSON_PLAN.md`.
   - Project `SLIDES.md` on the main screen for visual explanation.
   - Facilitate Activity 1 (Spot the Scam), the interactive quiz game, and Activity 3 (Safety Pledge) from `ACTIVITIES.md`.
3. **After Class:**
   - Send students home with their signed Safety Pledge and the cheatsheet.
   - Encourage them to teach a younger sibling or parent one Golden Rule they learned!

---

## 🇱🇰 A Note on Getting Help

This workshop teaches students to always talk to a **trusted adult** first (a parent, guardian, teacher, or Code Club facilitator) whenever something online feels wrong. For serious concerns in Sri Lanka, the **National Child Protection Authority (NCPA) helpline is 1929** — a free, 24-hour number. Make sure students know an adult is always the first step, not a website or a stranger.
