## 🚀 Activity 2: Main Project — "Phase 2 Website Upgrade"

Your mission: use **vibe coding** to make real, working, understood improvements to your own website from the HTML/CSS workshops!

### 📋 Checklist & Requirements:

- [ ] **Level 1: Open Your Project**
  - Open your website files from the HTML/CSS workshops (or the fallback baseline at `css-workshop-grade-5-8/solution-kit/` if needed).

- [ ] **Level 2: Describe Your First Improvement**
  - Pick ONE specific thing to improve — not "make it better," but something concrete, e.g.:
    - "Make my profile picture have a glowing border that pulses slowly."
    - "Add a smooth fade-in animation when the page loads."
    - "Make my color palette feel more like a sunset (orange, pink, purple)."
    - "Make my navigation bar stick to the top of the page when I scroll."
  - Write your description here: ___________________________

- [ ] **Level 3: Generate, Read, and Test**
  - Get the AI-suggested code (via the facilitator-run tool, or an approved classroom tool).
  - **Before pasting it in:** read through it. Does it look like real HTML/CSS you recognize? Any new properties? Look them up or ask!
  - Paste it in, save, and refresh your browser. Did it work as expected?

- [ ] **Level 4: Understand It**
  - In your own words, explain what the new code does: ___________________________
  - If you can't explain it yet, ask the AI to explain it in simpler terms, or ask your facilitator.

- [ ] **Level 5: Repeat for a Second Improvement**
  - Choose a second improvement and repeat Levels 2-4.

- [ ] **Level 6: Reflection**
  - Did anything the AI suggested NOT work, or need fixing? What did you do about it?

## 🏆 Activity 3: Fast-Finisher Boss Quests! (For Speed Coders)

Finished Activity 2 already? Level up your AI literacy with these Boss Quests!

### ⚡ Quest A: The AI Fact-Check Challenge
Ask the class's supervised AI chatbot a factual question (e.g., "What is the tallest mountain in Sri Lanka?"). Then verify the answer using a reliable source (a textbook, encyclopedia, or a trusted website your facilitator approves). Did the AI get it right? Report your finding to the class.

### ⚡ Quest B: Design an AI Ethics Poster
Using what you've learned, sketch a poster (on paper or in your website as a new section!) teaching one AI ethics principle to others — for example "Don't Overshare with AI!" or "Always Double-Check AI Facts!" Use your own words and a simple illustration.

### ⚡ Quest C: The "Explain It Back" Challenge
Pick the AI-suggested code snippet you understand LEAST well from your Phase 2 upgrade. Ask the AI assistant to explain it "like I'm in Grade 5," in simple terms. Then try explaining it to a partner in your own words — did the simpler explanation help?

### ⚡ Quest D: Prompt Iteration Ladder
Start with a very plain image prompt (just a subject, like `"a bird"`). Generate it (facilitator-run), then improve it step-by-step through 3 rounds, adding one new detail (style, then setting, then mood) each time. Compare all 4 results — how much did the final image change from the first?
