## 🖥️ Slide 2: What is AI, Really?
- Is your phone's autocomplete "thinking"? No — it's **guessing based on patterns** from millions of examples.
- Generative AI is a supercharged version of that: predicting a good next word, pixel, or line of code.
- **It does NOT truly understand meaning, and it does NOT know for certain what's true.**

---

## 🖥️ Slide 3: What AI is Good (and Not So Good) At
| Good At ✅ | Not Good At (Yet) ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Fast first drafts | Guaranteeing 100% truth |
| Combining ideas creatively | Understanding YOUR specific project |
| Explaining things a new way | Replacing your own understanding |

**The Golden Rule:** *"AI is your assistant. You are still the author."*

## 🖥️ Slide 6: AI Ethics — Hallucination 🚩
- AI can confidently state things that are **completely wrong**.
- This is called a **hallucination**.
- **Rule:** Always verify important facts using a trusted, reliable source.

---

## 🖥️ Slide 7: AI Ethics — Bias & Privacy 🌍🔒
- **Bias:** AI learns from real-world data, which contains real-world bias — results aren't automatically fair or neutral.
- **Privacy:** Never type your full name + school, address, phone number, or passwords into an AI chatbot.

---

## 🖥️ Slide 8: AI Ethics — You're Still the Author ✍️
- Discussion: *"If an AI writes a whole website for you and you don't understand any of the code, did YOU build a website?"*
- **Rule:** Be able to explain any code or work you submit — AI-assisted is great, AI-replaced-your-understanding is not honest.

## 🖥️ Slide 10: Vibe Coding — The Loop 🔁
```text
1. DESCRIBE   → Tell the AI what you want, in plain English
2. GENERATE   → Let it suggest code
3. TEST       → Paste it in, save, refresh — does it work?
4. UNDERSTAND → Explain it in your own words
5. FIX/ITERATE → Still broken or confusing? Describe the problem, try again
```
