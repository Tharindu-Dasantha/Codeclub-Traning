# 🤖 Code Club Workshop: AI for Creativity, Coding & Responsible Use
> **Target Audience:** Grade 5-8 Students (Ages 10-14 in Sri Lanka)
> **Duration:** 2 Hours (120 Minutes)
> **Topic:** Generative AI for images, "vibe coding" with AI coding assistants, and AI ethics/responsible use
> **Prerequisites:** A personal website from the [HTML Workshop](../html-workshop-grade-5-8/) and [CSS Workshop](../css-workshop-grade-5-8/) — this session upgrades that project ("Phase 2")!

---

## 📌 Workshop Overview

Welcome to **AI for Creativity, Coding & Responsible Use**! This 2-hour interactive workshop is designed specifically for Sri Lankan Grade 5-8 students in Code Club.

Students have already built a personal website by hand (HTML) and styled it (CSS). Today, they learn to use **Artificial Intelligence as a creative and coding partner** — generating original AI images, and using **"vibe coding"** (describing what you want in plain English and having an AI assistant suggest the code) to upgrade their own website into **"Phase 2."**

But a powerful tool needs responsible judgment. Just as much time is spent on **AI ethics**: understanding that AI can be wrong (hallucinate), reflects the biases of its training data, should never be told personal information, and — most importantly — that the student remains the author and thinker, with AI only ever assisting, never replacing their own understanding.

## 🎯 Key Learning Objectives

By the end of this 2-hour session, students will be able to:
1. Explain, in simple terms, what generative AI actually does (recognizing patterns from huge amounts of training data to predict a good next word/pixel) and that it is **not** magic, and does not truly "understand" or "think" like a person.
2. Write an effective **image-generation prompt** using the Subject + Style + Setting + Mood formula, and iterate on a prompt based on the result.
3. Explain what **"vibe coding"** means: describing a desired feature or style in plain English to an AI coding assistant, then **reading, testing, and understanding** the code it suggests rather than blindly pasting it in.
4. Apply vibe coding to make at least 2 real, working improvements to their own HTML/CSS website.
5. Identify at least 3 **AI ethics principles**: AI can be factually wrong ("hallucination") and must be verified, AI can reflect bias from its training data, and personal information should never be shared with an AI chatbot.
6. Explain why **they, not the AI, remain the author** of their project — and why understanding the code you use matters, even when AI helped write it.

## 🛠️ Required Setup & Hardware

### For Students:
* Desktop/Laptop with a modern web browser.
* Their saved website files from the HTML/CSS workshops (or this session's [`starter-kit/`](starter-kit/) fallback baseline noted above).
* A text editor (VS Code, Notepad++, or whatever was used in the HTML/CSS sessions).

### For Instructor / Facilitator — ⚠️ Please Read Before Class:
* **Age & account policy matters here.** Most mainstream generative AI tools (AI chatbots and AI image generators) have Terms of Service requiring users to be 13-18+, or require parental/organizational consent for minors. **Do not have individual students sign up for personal accounts on these platforms.**
* **Recommended approach:** The facilitator uses **one adult-supervised account** (ideally an official school/organization education account with content filtering enabled, if available) and **projects it on the main screen**. Students call out or write down the prompts they want tried; the facilitator runs them live. This keeps the session hands-on and interactive without creating individual accounts for minors.
* If your organization has an approved, education-licensed AI tool with proper age-appropriate safeguards already set up for students (e.g. a school Microsoft/Google Workspace for Education AI feature), that is the preferred option for any part of the session where students do get hands-on access — confirm this in advance with your Code Club coordinator.
* Whichever tool you choose, **check its current Terms of Service and content-safety settings before the session** — enable strict/family content filters wherever available.
* This whole situation is itself a great live example of **Golden Rule 5 from the [Online Safety Workshop](../online-safety-workshop-grade-5-8/)**: think before you click, and check with a trusted adult about the rules before using a new online tool!

## 🚀 How to Run This Session

1. **Before Class:**
   - Decide which AI image tool and AI coding-assistant tool you'll use, following the safety notes above, and confirm they work on the venue's network.
   - Make sure students bring/can access their HTML/CSS website files from earlier sessions.
   - Review `LESSON_PLAN.md` for timing and `ACTIVITIES.md` for the prompt-writing exercises.
2. **During Class:**
   - Follow the 120-minute timeline in `LESSON_PLAN.md`.
   - Project `SLIDES.md` on the main screen for visual explanation.
   - Facilitate Activity 1 (Prompt Like a Pro) and Activity 2 (Phase 2 Website Upgrade) from `ACTIVITIES.md`.
3. **After Class:**
   - Celebrate the upgraded "Phase 2" websites — this closes the loop on a project students have now built, styled, and enhanced across three sessions!
