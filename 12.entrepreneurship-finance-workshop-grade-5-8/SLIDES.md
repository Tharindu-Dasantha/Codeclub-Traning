## 🖥️ Slide 4: The PSCU Framework 🧩
```text
PROBLEM   → What real problem are you solving?
SOLUTION  → What is your product or service?
CUSTOMER  → Who exactly would want/need this?
UNIQUE    → What makes yours different or better?
```
- **Check Question:** *"Why is 'everyone' a bad answer for Customer?"* (Too vague!)

---

## 🖥️ Slide 5: Money Matters — The 4 Basics 💰
| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue** | Total money coming IN |
| **Cost** | Total money going OUT |
| **Profit** | Revenue − Cost (what you keep) |
| **Price** | What you charge for ONE unit |

---

## 🖥️ Slide 6: Worked Example — Friendship Bracelets
```text
Price: Rs. 150   Cost: Rs. 50   Profit per bracelet: Rs. 100

Sell 20:
  Revenue = 20 x Rs. 150 = Rs. 3,000
  Cost    = 20 x Rs. 50  = Rs. 1,000
  Profit  = Rs. 2,000
```
- **Bonus - Break-Even:** Fixed cost ÷ Profit per unit = units needed to sell just to cover your starting cost.

## 🖥️ Slide 10: The Dragon's Den Pitch Structure 🎤
```text
1. HOOK        → Grab attention
2. PROBLEM     → The real problem
3. SOLUTION    → Your product/service
4. CUSTOMER    → Who it's for
5. THE NUMBERS → One key number
6. THE ASK     → What you want from your Dragons
```
- **Practice in Pitch Pods:** groups of 4-5, 60-90 seconds each, one comment + one question per pitch.
