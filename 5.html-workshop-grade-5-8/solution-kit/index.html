🐘 About My Maze Game

In Jungle Maze Escape, you help a baby elephant find its way out of a tricky jungle maze. Avoid the sneaky leopard, collect bananas for bonus points, and race the clock!

  • Goal: Guide the elephant from the entrance to the glowing exit gate.
  • Danger: Touching the leopard sprite sends you back to the start!
  • Bonus: Collect all 3 hidden bananas for extra points.

🕹️ How to Play

  1. Move: Use the Arrow Keys to walk Up, Down, Left, and Right.
  2. Dodge: Watch out for the leopard patrolling the maze paths!
  3. Win: Reach the green exit to complete the level!
