## 🖥️ Slide 2: Would You Do This in Real Life?
- *"If a stranger on the street offered you candy and asked for your home address, would you give it to them?"* → No!
- Every time we overshare online, we're doing exactly that — except the "crowd" can be millions of strangers.
- **The Internet is a City:** 🏛️ Libraries (educational sites) | 🏪 Shops (games, apps) | 🌆 Dark Alleys (scams, fake sites) — know the difference!

---

## 🖥️ Slide 3: The 5 Golden Rules ⭐
1. 🔒 Keep Personal Info Private
2. 🔑 Use Strong, Secret Passwords
3. 🤔 Think Before You Click
4. 💛 Be Kind Online, Report Unkindness
5. 🗣️ Tell a Trusted Adult

---

## 🖥️ Slide 4: Golden Rule 1 — Keep Personal Info Private 🔒
- **Never share:** home address, phone number, daily routine, passwords, location-revealing photos, full name + school together.
- **Ask yourself:** *"Would I be okay with this on a billboard my whole neighborhood can see?"*

---

## 🖥️ Slide 5: Golden Rule 2 — Password Power 🔑
- **Weak ❌:** `12345`, `password`, `yourname2011`
- **Strong ✅:** `Tea!Cup_47Sunny` — long, mixed characters, not a real word, unique per account.
- **Golden Rule:** Never share your password with anyone, ever — not even a friend!

---

## 🖥️ Slide 6: Golden Rule 3 — Think Before You Click 🤔
- **What is phishing?** A fake message tricking you into clicking a link or giving away info.
- **🚩 Red flags:** Urgency, too-good-to-be-true, weird links, poor spelling, asking for passwords, unknown senders.
  ```text
  "🎉 CONGRATULATIONS! You WON a free PS5!
  Click here within 10 minutes: bit.ly/free-ps5-claim
  Enter your name, address and phone number!"
  ```
- **Spot the flags:** Urgency + fake prize + suspicious link + asking for personal info!

## 🖥️ Slide 8: Golden Rule 4 — Be Kind, Report Unkindness 💛
- **Cyberbullying:** repeated mean comments, rumors, exclusion, sharing embarrassing content.
- **The T.H.I.N.K. Test before you post:**
  - **T**rue? **H**elpful? **I**nspiring? **N**ecessary? **K**ind?
- If experiencing or witnessing bullying: don't respond, screenshot it, tell a trusted adult.

---

## 🖥️ Slide 9: Online Strangers — Still Strangers! 🕵️
- Someone you've only met online is still a stranger, even if friendly.
- **Warning signs:** personal questions, "keep it secret," video call/meet-up requests, gifts.
- **The Rule:** Never meet an online-only contact in person without a trusted adult. Ever.

---

## 🖥️ Slide 10: Golden Rule 5 — STOP → BLOCK → TELL 🗣️
```text
1. STOP  → Don't reply. Don't click. Don't download.
2. BLOCK → Block or exit the account/site/message.
3. TELL  → Tell a trusted adult right away.
```
- You will NEVER be in trouble for telling the truth about feeling unsafe.
