# 🎮 Student Activities: Spot the Scam, Scenario Cards & Safety Pledge

> **Workshop:** Be Safe Online (Grade 5-8)
> **Instructions:** Complete Activity 1 to earn your Scam Spotter Badge, discuss Activity 2 in small groups, then sign your Safety Pledge in Activity 3!

---

## 🕵️ Activity 1: Spot the Scam!

For each message below, decide: is it **✅ Safe** or **🚩 Scam/Unsafe**? Circle your answer, then list the red flags you noticed.

### Message A:
> *"🎉 CONGRATULATIONS!! You are our LUCKY WINNER of a free PS5! Click here within 10 minutes to claim: bit.ly/free-ps5-claim-now. Enter your name, address and phone number to receive it!"*

**Your answer:** ✅ Safe / 🚩 Scam
**Red flags:** ___________________________

### Message B:
> *"Hey, it's Ravindu from your maths class! I forgot my textbook, can I borrow your notes tomorrow before class?"*

**Your answer:** ✅ Safe / 🚩 Scam
**Red flags (if any):** ___________________________

### Message C:
> *"URGENT: Your school account will be DELETED in 1 hour unless you verify your password here: schooI-portal-verify.com"*

**Your answer:** ✅ Safe / 🚩 Scam
**Red flags:** ___________________________

### Message D:
> *"Hi! I'm 12 too and I love the same games as you! Can you tell me your home address so I can send you a gift? Please don't tell your parents, it'll be our secret surprise! 😊"*

**Your answer:** ✅ Safe / 🚩 Scam
**Red flags:** ___________________________

### Message E:
> *"Reminder from Code Club: next Saturday's session starts at 9am, please bring your laptop charger."*

**Your answer:** ✅ Safe / 🚩 Scam
**Red flags (if any):** ___________________________

---

### 🔍 Answer Key (For Instructors):

- **Message A — 🚩 SCAM.** Red flags: urgency ("within 10 minutes"), too-good-to-be-true prize, shortened suspicious link, asking for personal information (name, address, phone).
- **Message B — ✅ SAFE.** A known classmate asking a normal, low-risk question. (Discuss: it's still smart to double-check it's really them if anything feels off!)
- **Message C — 🚩 SCAM.** Red flags: urgency + fear ("DELETED in 1 hour"), a misspelled/fake-looking web address (`schooI-portal-verify.com` — note the capital "I" pretending to be a lowercase "l"), asking to "verify" (enter) a password.
- **Message D — 🚩 SCAM / UNSAFE.** Red flags: a person only known online asking for a home address, offering a gift, and specifically asking to keep it secret from parents — a major stranger-danger warning sign.
- **Message E — ✅ SAFE.** A normal, expected message from a known, trusted organization with no requests for personal info, links, or urgency.

## 💬 Activity 2: Scenario Cards — What Would You Do?

Split into small groups. Each group discusses one or two scenarios and reports back their answer + reasoning to the class.

**Scenario 1:** *You're playing an online game and another player keeps sending you mean messages every time you lose a round. It's starting to happen every day.*
> What would you do? *(Suggested answer: don't respond/engage, mute or block the player, screenshot the messages, tell a trusted adult.)*

**Scenario 2:** *A pop-up appears on your screen saying "WARNING: Your device has 3 viruses! Click here to download Free Cleaner App now!"*
> What would you do? *(Suggested answer: don't click anything in the pop-up, close the browser/tab, tell an adult if unsure — real antivirus warnings don't work like this.)*

**Scenario 3:** *An online friend you've chatted with for a few weeks (who says they're your age) asks to video call, then asks to meet up in person this weekend without telling your parents.*
> What would you do? *(Suggested answer: say no, this is a major red flag; tell a trusted adult immediately; never agree to meet an online-only contact alone.)*

**Scenario 4:** *You get an email that looks like it's from a popular game asking you to "log in here" to claim a reward, but the web address looks slightly different from the real game's website.*
> What would you do? *(Suggested answer: don't click the link; go to the real website directly by typing the address yourself; report/delete the email.)*

**Scenario 5:** *A friend shares an embarrassing photo of a classmate in a group chat and asks you to forward it too.*
> What would you do? *(Suggested answer: refuse to forward it, ask your friend to take it down, tell a trusted adult — forwarding it would make you part of the cyberbullying.)*

## ✍️ Activity 3: My Online Safety Pledge

Fill in the blanks and sign your name — take this home and stick it somewhere you'll see it!

```text
     ⭐ MY ONLINE SAFETY PLEDGE ⭐

I, _____________________________, promise to:

  🔒 Keep my personal information private —
     my address, phone number, and passwords
     are for me (and my family) to know, not
     the internet.

  🔑 Use strong, secret passwords, and never
     share them with anyone — not even a friend.

  🤔 Think before I click. If a message feels
     urgent, too good to be true, or asks for
     personal info, I will STOP and check with
     a trusted adult first.

  💛 Be kind online, and speak up if I see
     someone being unkind to others.

  🗣️ Tell a trusted adult right away if
     anything online ever makes me feel
     scared, confused, or uncomfortable —
     I will never be in trouble for telling
     the truth.

Signed: _______________________
Date:   _______________________
```
