## ⭐ The 5 Golden Rules of Internet Safety

| # | Rule | In Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | 🔒 **Keep Personal Info Private** | Never share your address, phone number, school name + full name together, or passwords |
| 2 | 🔑 **Use Strong, Secret Passwords** | Long, mixed characters, unique per account, never shared |
| 3 | 🤔 **Think Before You Click** | Watch for urgency, "too good to be true" offers, weird links |
| 4 | 💛 **Be Kind Online, Report Unkindness** | Use the T.H.I.N.K. test before posting; never join in on bullying |
| 5 | 🗣️ **Tell a Trusted Adult** | Always, whenever something feels wrong — no exceptions |

---

## 🔑 Building a Strong Password

**Weak passwords ❌:** `12345`, `password`, `yourname2011`, your pet's name, your birthday.

**Strong passwords ✅:**
- 10-12+ characters long.
- Mix of UPPERCASE, lowercase, numbers, symbols.
- Not a real word or personal fact.
- **Different for every account.**

**The Passphrase Trick:** 3 random words + a number + a symbol:
```text
Tea!Cup_47Sunny
```
Easy to remember, hard to guess!

## 🚩 Red Flags of a Scam or Phishing Message

1. **Urgency** — "Act now! Only 10 minutes left!"
2. **Too good to be true** — "You've WON a free prize!"
3. **Weird links** — `amaz0n-prizes-win.com` instead of `amazon.com`
4. **Poor spelling/grammar** — Real companies proofread.
5. **Asking for passwords or payment info** — No real company asks this way.
6. **Unknown sender + link/attachment** — From someone or an account you don't recognize.

> ⚠️ **Golden Rule:** If it feels urgent, too good to be true, or asks for personal info — STOP and ask a trusted adult before clicking anything.

## 🧠 The T.H.I.N.K. Test — Before You Post or Send

| Letter | Ask Yourself |
| :--- | :--- |
| **T** | Is it True? |
| **H** | Is it Helpful? |
| **I** | Is it Inspiring? |
| **N** | Is it Necessary? |
| **K** | Is it Kind? |

If the answer to any of these is "no" — don't send it.

## 🕵️ Online Strangers — Know the Warning Signs

Someone you've only met online is **still a stranger**, even if they seem friendly. Be alert if they:
- Ask personal questions (address, school, daily routine).
- Ask you to keep the friendship "secret" from parents.
- Ask to video call or meet in person.
- Offer gifts or money.

**The Rule:** Never agree to meet an online-only contact in person without a trusted adult. Ever.

---

## 🚦 STOP → BLOCK → TELL

When anything online feels wrong, scary, or confusing:

```text
   1. STOP    →  Don't reply. Don't click. Don't download.
   2. BLOCK   →  Block or exit the account/site/message.
   3. TELL    →  Tell a trusted adult right away.
```

You will never be in trouble for telling the truth about feeling unsafe.

---

## 🇱🇰 Who to Tell

1. A parent or guardian.
2. A teacher or your Code Club facilitator.
3. For serious concerns in Sri Lanka: **National Child Protection Authority (NCPA) helpline — 1929** (free, 24 hours).

## 🚨 Top 5 Things to NEVER Share Online

1. ❌ Your home address or daily routine.
2. ❌ Your passwords — to anyone, ever.
3. ❌ Photos that reveal your location (uniform, street signs, GPS tags).
4. ❌ Your full name + school name together, publicly.
5. ❌ Personal secrets someone online asks you to "keep just between us."
